Features

What an AMC contractor's office does every day, in one system.

Made from how maintenance actually runs on site: PM rounds, breakdown calls, work permits and the monthly report to the client.

AMC contracts and SLA hours

Contract period, value in AED, SAR or USD, PM visits per year, and response times for Emergency, High and Normal calls. Renewals are flagged 30 days ahead.

Equipment register

Chillers, AHUs, FCUs, package units, splits, VRF, cooling towers and pumps with tag, model, serial, TR, refrigerant, warranty, and age against design life.

PM calendar with HVAC checklists

Schedule a whole month in one click, by each unit's frequency. A PM closes only when every checklist item is ticked and the readings are recorded.

Complaints with live SLA timers

Every call shows the time left before the SLA is breached. The calls most at risk sit at the top of the dashboard.

Work permits on every job

Record the permit number on PM and breakdown jobs, for plants and industrial sites that work under a permit system.

Spare parts history

Which part went into which unit, when, and at what cost. Faults and parts that keep coming back are flagged.

Monthly client report

PM done against planned, complaints by fault type, SLA met, and parts used, for each client and month.

Client portal

Your client's facility manager logs in, sees only their own sites, raises complaints and checks PM history. No more calls asking "did you come?".

Questions

Short answers.

Do we need to install anything?

No. It opens in any browser on a computer, tablet or phone. We host it on a secure HTTPS address with backups.

Can our clients see other clients' data?

No. Each client login sees only that client's sites, equipment, complaints and reports. This is enforced in the database, not only on the screen.

We keep everything in Excel today. Do we start from zero?

No. Send us your client list, sites and equipment list in Excel, and we load them before you start.

Which currencies and countries does it support?

Contracts and parts can be in AED, SAR, USD, QAR, OMR, KWD or BHD. It is built for contractors across the Gulf and Iraq.

Is it in Arabic?

English today. An Arabic interface is on the list above.

Can it match our own PM checklists?

Yes. Each equipment type comes with a standard HVAC checklist, and we change it to match the checklists in your contracts.
