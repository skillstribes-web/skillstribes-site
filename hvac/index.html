Features

HVAC maintenance software for everything an AMC contractor's office does every day.

Made from how maintenance actually runs on site: PM rounds, breakdown calls, work permits and the monthly report to the client.

Contracts and clients

Every AMC in one place

  • Contract period, value in AED, SAR or USD, and PM visits per year
  • Response time (SLA) for Emergency, High and Normal calls, set per contract
  • Renewals flagged 30 days before the contract ends
  • Sites for each client: malls, factories, hospitals, plants

Equipment

A history for every unit

  • Chillers, AHUs, FCUs, package units, splits, VRF, cooling towers, pumps
  • Tag, model, serial, TR, refrigerant, install date and warranty
  • Age against design life, so you see which units are near the end
  • One page per unit with every PM, breakdown and part, and a QR tag

Maintenance

PM and breakdowns under control

  • Schedule a month of PM in one click, by each unit's frequency
  • HVAC checklists and readings; a PM closes only when the checklist is complete
  • Complaints with live SLA timers and a technician on each job
  • Work permit number on every PM and breakdown job
  • Spare parts recorded against the unit and the job

Reports and control

See problems before the client does

  • Dashboard: PM due and overdue, open calls, SLA at risk
  • Repeat faults and repeat parts flagged automatically
  • Monthly report per client: PM done vs planned, SLA met, parts used
  • Client portal: each client sees only their own sites and raises complaints

Questions

Short answers.

Do we need to install anything?

No. It opens in any browser on a computer, tablet or phone. We host it on a secure HTTPS address with backups.

Can our clients see other clients' data?

No. Each client login sees only that client's sites, equipment, complaints and reports. This is enforced in the database, not only on the screen.

We keep everything in Excel today. Do we start from zero?

No. Send us your client list, sites and equipment list in Excel, and we load them before you start.

Which currencies and countries does it support?

Contracts and parts can be in AED, SAR, USD, QAR, OMR, KWD or BHD. It is built for contractors across the Gulf and Iraq.

How is it priced?

A monthly subscription for your company. Send us the number of sites and units you maintain and we will send you a quote.

Is our data ours?

Yes. On request we give you a full copy of your data in Excel, at any time, including if you decide to stop.

Is it in Arabic?

English today. An Arabic interface is on the list above.

Can it match our own PM checklists?

Yes. Each equipment type comes with a standard HVAC checklist, and we change it to match the checklists in your contracts.
