How it works

Set up around your business, not a template.

  1. You share your information

    Services, prices, timings, locations and the questions customers usually ask. We write it up and you approve it.

  2. We connect your WhatsApp

    Your WhatsApp Business number is set up on the official WhatsApp Business Platform, with your business name.

  3. The assistant answers

    It replies at any hour from your approved information and collects booking or enquiry details.

  4. Your team takes over

    Requests come to your team, and any chat can be handed to a person when the customer asks or the assistant is not sure.

What it can do

  • Answer questions about your services, prices, timings and location
  • Take appointment, booking, quote or complaint details and pass them to your team
  • Reply in English, Arabic or Urdu
  • Hand the chat to a person whenever it should

Good to know before you start

  • It answers only from the information you approve, and does not give medical, legal or financial advice.
  • It needs the official WhatsApp Business Platform. We help you set it up. Meta (WhatsApp) charges its own fee per message, paid by your business directly to Meta.
  • Ready in 5–7 working days, counted from the day Meta approves your number.

Questions

Common questions

Will it use my current WhatsApp number?

Yes, your WhatsApp Business number is connected to the official WhatsApp Business Platform. We check with you before any change, so your customers keep messaging the same number.

What if it does not know the answer?

It says so politely and passes the chat to your team. It does not make up prices or promises.

Can my staff still reply themselves?

Yes. Your team can take over any chat at any time.

Which businesses is it for?

Any business that gets questions on WhatsApp: clinics, transport and logistics, maintenance companies, shops, schools, real estate and more.

What does it cost?

We start with a free trial. The monthly fee depends on what the assistant needs to do, and we agree it with you in writing before the trial ends. WhatsApp's own per-message fee is paid by your business to Meta.
