    Privacy and safety

    Patient details stay small and protected.

    • Your own login. Every person has one. Passwords are stored scrambled, so nobody can read them back, including us.
    • Lock after mistakes. Five wrong passwords lock that login for 10 minutes.
    • Secure connection. The app runs over HTTPS.
    • Only what booking needs. Name, age, gender, phone, doctor, date and time. No diagnoses, prescriptions or test results.
    • Separate from other clinics. Each clinic's data sits in its own database.
    • Backups, and your data is yours. When a pilot ends you get a copy of your data and we delete ours.

    The app is an appointment tool, not a medical record system. Clinics remain responsible for following the rules that apply to patient information where they work.

    Questions

    Short answers about our clinic appointment software.

    Do staff or patients install anything?

    No. The app opens in any browser, on a computer or a phone.

    Can patients book online themselves?

    Not in the first version. Reception books, as it does today. If you want patient self-booking, tell us and we will plan it with you.

    Are reminders sent automatically?

    The app prepares each reminder and opens it in WhatsApp, and your staff press send, so you stay in control. Automatic sending can be added on request.

    What does it cost?

    The first 30 days are a free pilot. After that there is a simple monthly fee, which we agree with you in writing before it starts.

    Can we ask for changes?

    Yes. During the pilot we meet once a week to collect what your team needs, and afterwards you can still ask.

    What if we want to stop?

    You can stop at any time. You receive a copy of your data and we delete ours.

    Does the AI see our patients?

    Only if you ask us to switch it on, and even then it never receives patient names or phone numbers. By default it is off.

    Does it work for physiotherapy clinics?

    Yes. You can book a course of daily sessions in one step, for example 10, 14 or 42 sessions with Sundays skipped, and each visit shows "Day 4 of 10". The app does not store diagnoses, medicines or exercises.

    Which language is the app in?

    English today.
