About us

A small software company that starts from the field.

SkillsTribes was started by an engineer who works in gas turbine power plant maintenance.

Maintenance runs on details: work permits, PM rounds, breakdowns, spare parts and the report at the end of the month. Large plants manage them with systems like SAP. Most contractors and smaller teams still use Excel sheets, registers and WhatsApp groups, because the big systems are too heavy and too expensive for them.

We build focused web software for that gap: easy to learn, priced for small and mid-size teams, and set up with your own data before you start. The same way of working goes into our appointment software for clinics.
