    How it works

    Four steps, no extra work for your team.

    1. Order comes in

      From your Shopify or WooCommerce store, or a row in your Google Sheet if you take orders on Instagram and WhatsApp.

    2. Customer gets WhatsApp

      Order number, items, total, address, and buttons to confirm, cancel or change the address. In Urdu or English.

    3. The answer is saved

      The order is marked Confirmed, Cancelled or "Address change" on its own. No one has to read chats and copy answers.

    4. No reply? Follow up

      If the customer does not answer in the time you choose, a reminder goes out, and the order waits in a separate list for your team to call.

    What you get

    • Setup on your store and your WhatsApp Business number, done by us
    • Message text in your words, in Urdu, English or both
    • A simple list of today's orders: confirmed, cancelled, waiting
    • Changes you ask for during the trial, for example a different reminder time or a new message

    Good to know before you start

    • Automatic WhatsApp messages need the official WhatsApp Business Platform. We help you set it up on your number.
    • Meta (WhatsApp) charges a small fee per message, about USD 0.015 per order-update message in Pakistan at today's rates. This is paid by the store directly to Meta, not to us.
    • It cannot stop every return. It does tell you which orders the customer confirmed before you pay the courier.

    Questions

    Common questions

    Do I need Shopify?

    No. It works with Shopify, WooCommerce, or a Google Sheet where your team writes the orders.

    Will the message come from my number?

    Yes. It comes from your store's WhatsApp Business number, with your store's name.

    Can customers reply in their own words?

    Yes. Button answers are saved on their own. Any other reply stays in your WhatsApp for your team to read.

    What does it cost?

    The first 30 days are a free trial, and nothing is charged by us. After that there is a monthly fee, which we agree with you in writing before the trial ends. WhatsApp's own per-message fee is paid by the store to Meta.

    Can we stop at any time?

    Yes. You keep your orders and your WhatsApp number.
